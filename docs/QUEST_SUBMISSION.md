## 4. Narrative

CIRCLA is not another stock dashboard or single-user trading bot.

It brings a familiar savings-circle behavior into Base's programmable equity ecosystem.

The social decision happens in Telegram, but the financial truth and enforcement happen onchain.

This makes collective ownership of tokenized stocks understandable, auditable, and composable.
